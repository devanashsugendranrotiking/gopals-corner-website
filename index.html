Our Story

Nearly fifty years of Malaysian Indian cooking

Gopal's Corner started in 1976 as a family kitchen serving the dishes Malaysia runs on: flaky roti canai, crisp dosa, rich curries and rice served on a banana leaf.

Today it is part of the Roti King family, cooking the same recipes for a new generation of diners in London and Kuala Lumpur. The recipes haven't changed, and neither has the welcome.
