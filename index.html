Our Story

Gopal's Corner began in 1976 as a small family kitchen serving the food Malaysia runs on: roti off the griddle, thosai, rich karis and rice on a banana leaf.

Today it is part of the Roti King family, and the recipes are cooked for a new generation in London. They haven't changed, and neither has the welcome.

From our family to yours.
